02 / METHOD

The classroom
behaves like a studio.

You learn a concept, make something with it, get feedback, revise, and then do it again at a higher level.

WEEK 01Learn the tool

Core concepts are introduced through small, focused exercises.

WEEK 02+Build under constraint

Projects force decisions instead of passive note-taking.

REVIEWCritique the result

Work is checked for clarity, structure and technical correctness.

REPEATMake the next one better

Confidence comes from useful repetition, not memorizing definitions.
